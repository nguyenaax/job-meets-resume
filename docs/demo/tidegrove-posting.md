## About the role

Tidegrove Analytics builds an exposure-management platform for mid-size enterprises. The Vulnerability Intelligence team decides which newly disclosed vulnerabilities matter to our customers, and how urgently, before they ask.

## Responsibilities

- Track newly disclosed CVEs from NVD, vendor advisories and researcher write-ups, and triage which ones affect products in our customers' environments.
- Grade the exploitation evidence for each priority CVE: proof-of-concept code, in-the-wild reports, CISA KEV listings and EPSS scores, and record a confidence level with the sources behind it.
- Write the weekly vulnerability brief for customers, and a same-day flash note when a priority CVE is confirmed exploited.
- Keep the vulnerability intelligence records in Jira current as new evidence appears, and re-grade CVEs when it changes the picture.
- Answer analyst questions from the detection engineering team and customer security teams, and feed their requests back into what the team tracks.
- Automate collection from NVD, the KEV catalog and vendor advisory feeds with Python.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in vulnerability intelligence, cyber threat intelligence or vulnerability management.
- Working knowledge of CVSS v3.1 and v4.0, EPSS and the CISA KEV catalog.
- Clear writing for both technical and executive readers.
- Python for scripting and data collection.

## Preferred

- Experience reading exploit code and proof-of-concept repositories.
- Familiarity with MITRE ATT&CK.
